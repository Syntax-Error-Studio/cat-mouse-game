## 1. Trigger formula (G0.3R-validated composites, no re-tuning)

Baseline DENSE (G0.3S trigger), then only semantically-meaningful discrete
boolean combinations — no grid search, no fixture numeric threshold:

```
prefilter        = playing ∧ currentPlayer=mouse ∧ mouseHasButter
DENSE            = prefilter ∧ interceptableRouteRatio==0 ∧ bestInterceptMargin>0
C1 (offAxis)     = DENSE ∧ ¬catOnGateAxis
C2 (gatePos)     = DENSE ∧ criticalGateMargin>0
C3 (off+gate)    = DENSE ∧ ¬catOnGateAxis ∧ criticalGateMargin>0
```

For the G4 pair: BAD (3,5)/(4,7) has `{ratio:0, margin:6, axis:false,
gateMargin:6}`; GOOD (3,7)/(4,7) has `{ratio:0, margin:4, axis:true,
gateMargin:4}`.

## 2. G4 critical extension points

The two load-bearing mouse roots on the corrected line:

| point | line | DENSE | C1 | C2 | C3 |
|-------|------|-------|----|----|----|
| BAD (3,5)/(4,7) | production CAN_FORCE (must be punished) | trigger | trigger | trigger | trigger |
| **GOOD (3,7)/(4,7)** | RRRR ALL_PATHS_AVOID (corrected-line point) | **trigger** | **NO** | **trigger** | **NO** |

**The structural deadlock:** GOOD sits exactly ON the gate axis
(`catOnGateAxis=true`, col 7 = the interceptor column). Every sparse variant
that uses `¬catOnGateAxis` (C1/C3) **drops the GOOD critical point** — gate A
fails. Every variant that keeps GOOD (DENSE/C2) still triggers at ~50% global
rate — gate C fails. There is no G0.3R semantic composite that both retains the
critical point and is sparse.

## 5. Estimated cost (feasibility only — NOT correctness truth)

Using G0.3S measured ratios: d1+ext1 ≈ 10.65 nodes/trigger (34,948/3,282);
Q+3-scale ≈ 2,180 nodes/trigger (878,738/403, G4).

| composite | triggers | est d1+ext1 nodes | est Q+3-scale nodes | est Q+3-scale @ G4T2 |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| DENSE | 1,079 | ≈11,490 | ≈2.35M | ≈312k |
| C1/C3 | 572 | ≈6,091 | ≈1.25M | ≈240k |

Even the sparsest composite would cost ≈240k nodes for G4T2 alone at Q+3 scale
— far beyond a 500ms budget. Targeting the §5 reference (≈7.6 triggers total)
would require a further 100× selective reduction that no G0.3R semantic
boolean provides; attempting it would mean tuning to the G4 fixture (forbidden
by §2/§10).

## 6. G1 control (trigger statistics only, no re-search)

G1 forced corpus responses under DENSE: G1T2 14/14 (the mouse-forced T2 root —
64/64 plans forced) and G1T3 34/34 all trigger; C1 reduces to 12 and 33 — i.e.
even on the PROVEN mouse-forced control the trigger stays dense. Consistent
with §5's cost picture: at these densities leaf-level interception quiescence
equals nearly whole-tree deepening, including on the control.

## 7. Verdict

**`SPARSE_LEAF_TRIGGER = NOT_FEASIBLE`**

**`LEAF_LEVEL_QUIESCENCE_PATH = EXHAUSTED`**

Grounds (spec §6, all four gates):
- **A FAIL**: every composite that is sparse at all (C1/C3) drops the G4 GOOD
  critical point (GOOD is on the gate axis; `catOnGateAxis=true` is precisely
  why the RRRR line is safe, and precisely why off-axis sparsification removes
  it). The "sparse" cut is the wrong cut by construction.
- **B FAIL**: minimum achievable G4T2 trigger count = 110 (C1/C3) — 13-14×
  above the single-digit reference (goal ~7.6 triggers from the 431x workload
  need; nothing gets within an order of magnitude).
- **C FAIL**: best global trigger rate = 26.64%, 53× above the ≤0.5% target;
  DENSE = 50.26% (unique-leaf) / 64.95% (search-check, G0.3S).
- **D PASS** only (no numeric threshold tuning was used) — insufficient.
- Estimated Q+3-scale cost for the sparsest composite ≈ 1.25M nodes globally /
  240k nodes on G4T2 alone.

Per §7, leaf-level interception quiescence is closed for this line:
- no further threshold tuning,
- no more Q+3 runs,
- no node-cap patching (a cap would re-buy the G0.3B failure mode: selective
  work that cannot complete in budget),
- no G0.3S.1/S.2 continuation.

**Next direction (recorded, NOT implemented this phase):** **ROOT / PLAN-LEVEL
BOUNDED TACTICAL PROBE**. The expensive interception analysis can only be done
a handful of times per cat turn (once per CAPTURED root action, or once per
candidate whole-turn plan) — never once per horizon leaf. The G0.3S Q+3 result
proved the *information* exists at depth (RRRR escapes, ArrowLeft mates); the
delivery mechanism must be a bounded per-plan probe at the root level, not a
per-leaf quiescence extension.
