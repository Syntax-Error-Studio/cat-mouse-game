## 0. Method

- Every cat-turn root of GAMES 1/2/4/5 fully parsed; GAME3 tail only (T9–T10).
- Forced-loss probe: each legal root action searched with a fresh context,
  full alpha-beta window, deadline OFF, at child depths 1→2→3 (≈ root d2→d3→d4),

  `mate=mouse` at some completed depth.
- Every value is bit-identical to serial truth (the forced-branch methodology
  was validated in G0.3N against NO_AB full-window expansion).
