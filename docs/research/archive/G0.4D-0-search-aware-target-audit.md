## 2. Search leaf semantics (spec 4) — what the search actually needs

Real read of `expectiminimax.ts` / `hardTurnPlanner.ts`:
- The leaf evaluator (`leafEvaluator` → `evaluateLeaf`) is applied at: horizon leaves (L1383/1419), repetition leaves (L1341), no-legal-action leaves (L1047), **budget-cutoff leaves (L1450)** and **deadline-cutoff leaves (L1463)** — the latter two being the dominant ValueNet leaf sites in a time-boxed search.
- `maximizing = state.currentPlayer === PieceType.Cat` (L1052): cat = MAX, mouse = MIN. The leaf value is ALWAYS cat-favored (higher = better for cat); MIN/MAX is the search's job (`maximizing ? cmp>0 : cmp<0`).
- **The leaf evaluator is called on ARBITRARY search-frontier states** — cat-to-move AND mouse-to-move, on-policy AND off-policy/adversarial, chance-adjacent, and states the canonical policy never visits.
- **The real requirement is CAT-FAVORED ORDERING over OFF-POLICY / ADVERSARIAL FRONTIER STATES.**
- Current V_pi learns `E[terminal | s, fixed pi = (hard cat, greedy mouse)]` — a global terminal-outcome classification over ON-POLICY states. **Semantically different** from what the leaf needs.

## 5. Depth benchmark (spec 8) — node-cap feasibility wall

Bounded 500k node cap, no deadline, 15 cat-turn states:

| depth | completion | nodesMean | nodesP95 | wallMean | wallP95 | mateRate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D2 | 100% | 21,394 | 164,332 | 12.5 s | 133 s | 26.7% |
| D3 | 100% | 191,744 | **500,000 (cap)** | 52.3 s | 292 s | 26.7% |
| D4 | 100% | 366,819 | **500,000 (cap)** | 94.3 s | 253 s | 33.3% |

- **D2 is the only depth with clean completion (nodesP95 under the cap).**
- **D3/D4 hit the 500k node cap at the p95** — their "completion=100%" is budget-truncation, NOT exact minimax. Their values are cap-bound approximations.
- The fully-unbounded no-deadline teacher is computationally infeasible: D2 alone averaged 12.5-32 s/state (20-95 s under load), with a p95 tail of 130-296 s. D3/D4 at scale are prohibitive (minutes per state).
- **Honest report**: the deeper (D3/D4) teacher labels would be budget-truncated, not exact. Only D2 is clean.

## 15. Recommendation (spec 21) — NONE

The evidence is insufficient to recommend a next target:
1. **Teacher depth-stability FAIL** (D2→D3 top-action 0.375, tau −0.258) — deeper-search teacher labels are not reliable enough to train on.
2. **TT value/mate mismatch (23/24)** — a search-correctness concern must be resolved first (§12).
3. **T1 raw-scale risk = MATERIAL** — raw scalar is not a clean regression target.
4. **T2 pairwise**: promising in principle (matches search's ordering need, low OOD, relative scale) BUT has a 30.6% tie rate on cat siblings and mouse-MIN sibling pairs were not captured; also inherits the teacher depth-stability / TT concerns.
5. **Computational infeasibility**: even D2 teacher is 12-30 s/state; a pilot dataset would be extremely expensive.

Per §21 ("如果证据不够：NONE — 不要为了继续项目强行选 target"):
