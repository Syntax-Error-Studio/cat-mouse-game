## 1. Design — Lazy turn-boundary search

The lazy prototype (`src/game/ai/turnLazy.ts`) reuses the legacy atomic-action
search but compresses WITHIN-turn duplication by memoizing turn-boundary
resolution: instead of expanding every atomic step sequence, it caches the
turn-end state results of repeated sub-trees and replays them (`withinTurnHits`
/ memo probes). This is the "lazy" variant of the G0.3K turn-boundary DAG: it
keeps the legacy MAX/MIN/EXPECT loop but avoids re-expanding the same
within-turn sub-tree twice.

Key diagnostics exposed: `generatedTransitions`, `expandedNodes`,
`orderingSimulations`, `withinTurnProbes` / `withinTurnHits`,
`turnTTExactHits` / `turnTTStores`, `skippedByAlphaBeta`, `potentialChildren`.

> **G0.3L-R1 correction (was §1 in the earlier draft):** the G0.3K EAGER
> variant's cost is NOT "pre-expansion waste". `DAG_NODES_BUILT ==
> DISTINCT_CONSUMED` (unused = 0%). The real eager cost is re-resolution
> (`RESOLVE_VISITS / distinctConsumed ≈ 2.36x d1, 4.55x d2` on C). The lazy
> prototype was written to avoid exactly that re-resolution cost.

## 5. Within-turn memo shadows turn-TT

`turnTTExactHits` are ≤ 0.11% of `turnTTStores` on the d2 3-way runs — the
within-turn memo makes the turn-TT exact reuse essentially redundant for the
lazy prototype. Lazy Alpha-Beta is far weaker than legacy at the same depth
(e.g. A g4t2 d2: lazy `ab=536` vs legacy `ab=1333`; lazy `abSkip=1180`), which
is why lazy node counts explode at d2 despite the memo.
