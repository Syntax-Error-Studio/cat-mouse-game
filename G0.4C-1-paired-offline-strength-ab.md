**Critical:** ValueNet has **734 cd0 turns (main search failed depth-1)** vs

leaves, ValueNet frequently fails to complete depth-1 and falls back to
`catAiMove` (which uses evaluateForCat anyway). ValueNet also searches fewer
depth-2+ nodes (cd2 1143 vs 2025). This is the primary mechanism of the
(statistically insignificant) deficit.
