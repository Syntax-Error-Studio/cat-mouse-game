(G1 B2 14 forced = PROVEN_MOUSE_WIN; G4 D 24 NOT_FORCED = NOT_PROVEN_FORCED_AT_PROBE_DEPTH).
No production changes, no evaluator/weight changes, no commits. Forensic only.
Tooling: `g03r.mts` (geometry kernel, evidence `g03r-out.json`), `g03r2.mts`
(SIMPLE vs EXACT_LOCAL gates), `g03r3.mts` (A+B+C-only cost).

**Time model (turn-aware):** arrival time of an actor on a cell is computed over
REAL turn windows with actual side-to-move — the side to move uses its remaining
movesLeft first, the opponent's full window elapses before the other actor may
move; mouse window = carrying ? 3 : 4, cat window = 4, strictly alternating.
Intercept margin(cell) = catArr − mouseArr (positive = cat would arrive later).

## 2. Route / gate structural explanation (3,5 → 3,7)

The mouse→hole corridor is **identical** in both states:
`[(4,7),(5,7),(6,7),(6,8),(7,8)]`, goal = 4 carrying steps. The critical gates
(cells on every shortest route) are **(4,7) (5,7) (6,8) (7,8)**.

What the turn-aware model adds over raw `catToCorridor`:

1. **Gate axis control (structural, not distance):** at (3,7) the cat is **on
   column 7 — the same column as gates (4,7) and (5,7)** → `catOnGateAxis=true`.
   SIMPLE cat BFS: (3,7)→(4,7)=1 step, (5,7)=2, (6,7)=3. The cat can park on the
   gate axis and, on every mouse move, step into an intercept square. At (3,5)
   the cat must detour (3 steps just to (4,7); (5,7)=4, (6,7)=5) — off-axis.
2. **Escape cone collapse (the decisive composite):** one mouse turn reachable
   next-cat boundaries: **242 (BAD) → 24 (GOOD)** — a 10× collapse: with the cat
   hugging the gate axis, the mouse's legal next-turn room implodes. Of the
   goal-threatening boundaries (mouse carrying, ≤ one mouse window from the hole),
   G4-BAD leaves **28/29 uncovered** (cat cannot answer in 4 moves: no capture,
   no gate-intercept) = 97% escape; G4-GOOD leaves **0/3 uncovered** — every
   goal-threat boundary is answerable → 100% respondable.
3. **Why this is turn-aware, not just distance:** naive `catToCorridor` (3 vs 1)
   only separates by closeness; the TIME model shows the mechanism — although the
   cat is *never* strictly earlier than the mouse on any single corridor cell
   (`interceptableRouteRatio=0` on both), the GOOD cat's 1-step gate access plus
   axis control lets it *respond* to every mouse continuation (respondable 100%),
   i.e. the cat can reach an intercept square within its next turn regardless of
   which route the mouse takes. The BAD cat loses the race on 28 of 29 mouse
   continuations.

**Structural verdict:** right-shifting two columns moves the cat from column 5
(off corridor axis) to column 7 (on the axis of gates (4,7)/(5,7)); SIMPLE gate
distances 3/4/5/6 → 1/2/3/4; the mouse escape cone collapses 242→24 and
uncovered-escape 97%→0%. The flip is axis/gate control — not a distance tweak,
not a box, not a tunnel.

## 6. SIMPLE vs EXACT_LOCAL

- SIMPLE cat BFS (no push-box) on the G4 gates: BAD 3/4/5/6(→(4,7)..(6,8)) vs
  GOOD 1/2/3/4 — clean separation, no box lies between the cat and the gate
  axis (both boards have 12 boxes; none on row 3/col 7 axis).
- EXACT_LOCAL (legal-actions incl. push-box / trap reclaim, up to 4 steps) from
  a mouse-turn root is inapplicable — the cat has not moved yet (EXACT 4-set is
  empty on mouse-turn states; observed capture distances "INF" for both).
- D/E response uses EXACT_LOCAL per next-cat boundary (cat-turn states, valid):
  it drives the respondable 3%→100% separation; push-box transitions are
  included there and do not erode the GOOD respondable=3/3 (axis gates are
  reachable without pushing).
- **Conclusion: on G4, SIMPLE suffices for the gate/margin signal; EXACT_LOCAL
  matters only for the D/E respondable component (cat-turn boundaries), where it
  is already used. Box/push does not change the G4 verdict.**

## 7. Verdict

**`INTERCEPTION_GEOMETRY = SEARCH_TRIGGER_CANDIDATE`** (§13-B)

Supporting (G4 + independent corpus):
- G4 causal pair separates **decisively** on the turn-aware composite
  `uncoveredEscapeRatio` (97%→0%) and `respondableRatio` (3%→100%), plus the
  structural `catOnGateAxis` and a 10× mouse reachable collapse — with a concrete
  mechanism (gate-axis control in the time model), not a distance coincidence.
- The 35-state exact corpus independently confirms the *same* mechanism:
  `interceptableRouteRatio=0 ∧ bestInterceptMargin>0` ⟺ PROVEN_MOUSE_WIN (11/11
  vs 0/24) — a cheap, rule-exact, state-only discriminator (A+B+C = 0.73× leaf).

Caveats (why trigger, not evaluator feature; why not NOT_GENERAL_ENOUGH):
- G2/G5 do NOT support any metric (1/3 §9 gate) — but their "deep-preferred"
  labels are G0.3O **UNRESOLVED d3 hints**, not proven escapes; the geometry
  cannot be exonerated or convicted on them this phase. Honest status: **2 of 3
  evidence sources positive (G4 pair + 35-state corpus), G2/G5 unknown/negative**.
- Cost: D/E bundle is 647× leaf and needs `enumerateFullTurnLegacy` (one-mouse-
  turn local expansion) → **exactly the §13-B profile**: worth triggering extra
  exact follow-through ONLY at critical roots; not a per-leaf evaluator feature.
- A+B+C-only is cheap but on the G4 pair alone is weaker (margin 4 vs 6; ratio 0
  both) — its strength shows on the corpus, so its prototype value is as the
  *cheap pre-filter* that decides when to fire the expensive D/E trigger.

Prototype design for the next phase (NOT this phase, per §15): at a cat-turn mouse
root with mouseHasButter, compute A+B+C (cheap); if `interceptableRouteRatio=0 ∧
catOnGateAxis=false ∧ gateMargin>0` (i.e. the cheap geometry says the cat loses
the gate race), allocate extra bounded search effort along the mouse continuation
(move ordering / selective extension), **strictly preserving the last-completed-
depth fallback** (the G0.3B lesson: never let the trigger drop completedDepth to 0).

No production changes. No commits.
