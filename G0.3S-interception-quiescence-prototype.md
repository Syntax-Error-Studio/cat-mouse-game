## 8. Verdict

**`INTERCEPTION_QUIESCENCE = PROMISING_BUT_TOO_EXPENSIVE`**

Evidence for "promising":
- Q+3 (deadline OFF) **fixes the G4 causal failure**: selects the proven
  GOOD RRRR escape (first action ArrowRight) and drops BAD. A separate notice:
  the Q+3 root-actions dump also exposed ArrowLeft as `mate=mouse −999,988`
  (rank 4) — an *additional* exact-loss discovery on a non-selected action,
  NOT the RRRR plan and NOT the chosen move.
- G1 control does not invent an escape; deepens to the exact G0.3O d3 truth.
- G2/G5 are never CONTRADICT; G5 reproduces the d3 truth value exactly.
- Correctness is clean: 9/9 trigger-free roots bit-identical; forced-loss
  mate/value preserved exactly; fallback to completed legacy d1 100% intact
  (no partial-selective leak; completedDepth never inflated).

Evidence for "too expensive":
- Minimum effective extension is **Q+3 ≥ 215s / 878k nodes per root** (§10:
  Q+1 and Q+2 do not repair the plan).
- Trigger is **not sparse (96.4%)** → quiescence degenerates to whole-frontier
  deepening; 403/403 leaves extend on G4 at every Q level.
- **Production gate FAILS**: at 100/500ms `usedSelective=0` — the selective
  attempt never completes; 0/12 repair BAD in either budget (though baseline
  fallback remains 100% sound).
- 26-root normalized regression: 6.3× nodes / 4.4× wall under 100ms.
- The failure mode is the SAME as G0.3B §3/§0 (selective extension collapses
  under the real budget — REAL Turn5 completedDepth 1→0 lesson): a dense
  trigger on tactical leaves destroys time-boxed completability.

NOT `WORTH_PRODUCTION_TRIAL` ((19) requires stable G4 fix within budget,
sparse trigger, 500ms completability — none hold). NOT `REJECTED` in the strict
§19 sense because a deadline-free quiescence DOES repair G4 (Q+3) and the
correctness/fallback surfaces are all clean — the honest label is
**promising in direction, prohibitive in cost with the minimum trigger**.
Future improvement is expressly OUT of scope this phase: the only plausible
cost fix would be a *sparse* trigger (e.g. G0.3R's off-axis/gate-margin
composite) or a hard extension-node cap — both are threshold experiments, which
§10 forbids tuning to the fixture here.

No production changes. No commits.
